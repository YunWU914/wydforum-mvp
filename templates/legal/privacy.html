This policy explains how {{ legal_company }} (“we”) handles personal data when you use {{ site_name }}. It is written for a global audience and references the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) where relevant.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: username, email address, password hash, account status and timestamps.
  • Content: topics, replies, reports and other material you submit.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser user agent, requested pages and timestamps, generated in server logs and used for security and rate limiting.
  • Preferences: interface language and session state.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, payment details, precise location, or government identifiers. Do not post them.

2. Why we use it

  • Create and secure accounts, confirm email addresses, and sign you in.
  • Publish and moderate content, and respond to reports and appeals.
  • Prevent spam, abuse and attacks, including rate limiting and CAPTCHA verification.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
  • Understand aggregate usage so we can improve the Service (no advertising profiles).

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We rely on contract (providing the Service you signed up for), legitimate interests (security, moderation, improving the Service, where not overridden by your rights), consent (where we ask for it, such as optional cookies), and legal obligation (records we must keep).

5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Service (hosting, email delivery, bot protection), under appropriate safeguards; with moderators who need it to review reports; when required by law; or as part of a reorganisation. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Retention

  • Account and content data: while your account exists, then deleted or anonymised within 30 days, except where we must retain records.
  • Email confirmation tokens: expire after {{ email_verification_hours }} hours and are stored only as hashes.
  • Rate-limit counters: at most 24 hours.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Reports and moderation records: up to 12 months for safety and appeals.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, confirmation tokens are hashed and single-use, sessions are protected with HTTP-only cookies, and we apply rate limits and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

To exercise a right, email {{ legal_contact_email }}. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within the time required by law.

The Service is not intended for children under 16 (or the minimum age in your country). If we learn that a child has created an account, we will remove it.
